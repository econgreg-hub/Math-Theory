WHAT THE RECORD DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
A public archived record identifies the disclosed content, attribution,
version and publication date. It supports a priority claim if the work is
correct and there is no earlier disclosure. It cannot prove absence of
prior work, guarantee citation/credit, or validate the source theorems.
SHA-256 hashes identify bytes; they do not independently establish a date.
